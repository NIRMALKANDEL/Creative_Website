## Why the first second matters

People decide in about a second whether to keep watching. Your hook is the *first frame and the first line*, not the third.

### Five hooks to try

1. **Start with the result.** Show the "after" before the "before".
2. **Ask a question your customer already asks.** "Is this worth it?"
3. **Break a common belief.** "You don't need to post every day."
4. **Point at something.** Movement towards the text pulls the eye.
5. **Talk to one person.** "If you run a café, watch this."

> A good hook makes a promise. The rest of the reel keeps it.

## Quick checklist

- Text on screen in the first frame
- Captions on, because most people watch with sound off
- One idea per reel
