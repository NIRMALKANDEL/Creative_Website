Questions

Straight answers.

What happens on the discovery call?{{faqPlus}}

We talk for 30 minutes about your brand, your goals and where your social is today. If we're a good fit, we'll send a short proposal. If we're not, we'll tell you, and point you somewhere useful. It's free and there's no obligation.

How long do you work with a brand?{{faqPlus}}

Most brands work with us on a monthly retainer with a minimum of three months, because social growth needs a few cycles of testing to show real results. The audit is a one-off session.

How do payments work?{{faqPlus}}

Retainers are billed monthly, in advance. One-off work like the audit is paid when you book. We'll confirm everything in writing before any work starts.

Do you guarantee followers or sales?{{faqPlus}}

No, and be careful of anyone who does. We guarantee the work: a clear plan, consistent quality content and honest monthly reporting. Growth follows good work, but no one controls the algorithm.

Why do you only take a few brands a month?{{faqPlus}}

Because good content needs attention. Keeping our list short means the people on your call are the people doing your work.
