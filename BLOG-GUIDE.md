# How to write a blog post — What's Beyond

You can add, edit and remove blog posts yourself, from any computer, at:

**https://YOUR-DOMAIN/admin**

## Logging in
1. Open the address above and press **Login with GitHub**.
2. Sign in with your GitHub account (the first time, press **Authorize**).
3. You'll see the list of your blog posts.

## Writing a new post
1. Press **New Blog post**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**: the headline. Choose it carefully before the first save, because it also becomes the post's web address.
   - **Publish date**: today to publish now. Pick a future date to **schedule** the post: it goes live that morning.
   - **Short description**: one or two sentences. This shows on the blog page and when someone shares the link on WhatsApp or Instagram.
   - **Category**: pick one. Readers can filter the blog by category.
   - **Tags** (optional): a few words people might search for, like *reels* or *captions*.
   - **Cover image** (optional): a landscape picture, about 1600 × 900, JPG/PNG/WebP. Please make it smaller than 500 KB first: drop it into **squoosh.app**, choose WebP, and download.
   - **Cover image description**: a few words describing the picture.
   - **Feature at the top of the blog**: switch on for your best post. The newest featured post gets the big banner.
   - **Last updated** (optional): set it when you make a real change to an older post.
   - **Post**: write here. Use the toolbar for **bold**, *italic*, links, headings, lists and quotes. To add a picture inside the post, press **+** and choose **Image**.
3. On the right you see a live preview of how the post will look.

## Publishing
- Press **Publish → Publish now**. The post is live on the website in about **1–2 minutes**.
- Want to save without showing it? Switch on **Draft (hide from website)**, then publish. It's saved but hidden until you switch Draft off.

## Finding posts
In the post list, use **Filter** (Drafts, Not drafts, Featured) or **Group by** category. Each row shows whether it's a draft, its date and when it was last edited.

## Editing or deleting
- Click any post in the list, change it, and press **Publish** again.
- To remove a post: open it, press **Delete entry** (top right).

## Good habits
- One idea per post; 400–900 words is plenty.
- Use headings so people can skim.
- End with a reason to book a discovery call.
- Only use photos you own or have permission to use.
